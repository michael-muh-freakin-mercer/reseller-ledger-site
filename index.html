FAQ

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes — import the .xlsx (File → Import in a blank sheet) and all formulas, dropdowns and charts carry over. It also opens directly in Excel and LibreOffice.

Are the platform fees accurate?

The defaults are typical US rates checked in November 2026 — but platforms change fees, and your rate can depend on category and seller status. The whole fee table is editable in one place, and every sale recalculates from it.

Is my sales data sent anywhere?

No. It's a spreadsheet file on your computer. There is no account, no sync, no tracking inside the product.

I sell outside the US — does it work?

The math works in any currency — treat the $ as yours. Update the fee table with your local rates (they're US defaults) and it's good.

What if I sell something as a bundle or a lot?

The Bundle Splitter tab divides the order total across items proportional to their solo value, so per-item profit stays accurate. Enter one Sales row per item with the allocated amounts.
