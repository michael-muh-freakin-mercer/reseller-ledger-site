Poshmark fees 2026: what sellers actually keep

November 2026 · These are typical US rates — Poshmark can change them, and your rate is whatever your receipt shows. Same defaults as our free fee calculator.

Poshmark's fee structure is simpler than most, which is why its one quirk trips people up: it charges a flat $2.95 on sales under $15 and a 20% commission on sales of $15 or more. There is no listing fee, no monthly fee and no separate payment-processing charge. But "simple" is not the same as "cheap" — Poshmark is one of the more expensive places to sell a mid-priced item, and the under-$15 flat fee is brutal on cheap ones.

The $15 trap

Notice the fourth row. A $14.99 sale keeps $12.04, but a $15.00 sale keeps $12.00. Any price from $15.00 to $15.04 actually earns you less than selling at $14.99, because the moment you cross $15 the flat $2.95 is replaced by a straight 20% — which is bigger than $2.95 until you pass roughly $14.75... and stays bigger forever after. Practically: if you're deciding between pricing a small item at $14.99 or $15.50, the $14.99 listing nets more until about $15.05. On low-value items, staying just under $15 is often the better play.

What Poshmark does not charge

  • No listing fee and no monthly subscription — listing is free, and you're only charged when something sells.
  • No separate payment-processing fee — unlike eBay, Mercari and Etsy, the 20% (or $2.95) is the whole seller fee. There's no added "2.9% + 30¢" on top.
  • Poshmark takes nothing from shipping. The buyer pays the $6.49 label (up to 5 lb) directly, and it doesn't run through your fee.

Two things that do eat into it

Shipping discounts you offer. If you offer the buyer a shipping discount to close a sale, that comes out of your earnings on top of the commission. A $2 "ship discount" on a $20 sale drops you from $16.00 to $14.00 — a 30% cut of the fee you thought you paid. Factor it in before you offer.

Heavier parcels. The buyer's label covers up to 5 lb; heavier items add $5 (5–10 lb) or $10 (10–15 lb) that the buyer pays, but which can make your listing less competitive. Nothing comes off your payout, but it affects whether the item sells at your price.

Poshmark vs the others, in one line

Poshmark's 20% is higher than eBay's ~13.6% + 30¢ and Mercari's 10%, and much higher than Depop's ~3.3% + 45¢ processing (Depop has no selling fee in the US). Where Poshmark wins is reach and simplicity: a free listing, one predictable fee, no processing surcharge, and a buyer-paid shipping label.
