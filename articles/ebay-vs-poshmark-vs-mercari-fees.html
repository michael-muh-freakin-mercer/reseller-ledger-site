eBay vs Poshmark vs Mercari vs Depop fees: what sellers actually keep

November 2026 · Rates below are typical US defaults — verify your own, they change. These are the same defaults used in our free fee calculator.

The same $35 sale does not pay you $35. Depending on where it sells, you might keep $34.55 or $26.44 — a difference of more than 30%. Here's the math, platform by platform, so you can price and cross-list with your eyes open.

Two traps that quietly eat margin

1. The under-$15 flat fee. Poshmark's $2.95 flat fee on orders under $15 is a 24.6% cut on a $12 sale — worse than its headline 20%. Cheap items belong on fee-free or low-fee platforms.

2. Fees on shipping charged. Most platforms charge their percentage on the item price plus shipping charged to the buyer. "Free shipping" baked into a higher item price can actually cost you more in fees than charging shipping separately — run both through the calculator before you decide.

What this means in practice

  • Cross-list everything, but know your floor price per platform — a $25 Poshmark offer can net less than a $23 Depop sale.
  • Log the fee per sale, not per month. "I made about $30" per item is how resellers end up with a busy year and nothing to show for it.
  • Check fees quarterly. Every platform on this list has changed its fee structure within the last two years.
